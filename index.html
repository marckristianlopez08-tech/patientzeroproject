01WHO WE ARE

Where curiosity meets practice.

Patient Zero is a student collective at the University of Toronto’s St. George campus. We create a space to explore the practical, diagnostic, and ethical sides of healthcare together.

Through interactive experiences, we connect students with clinicians, researchers, faculty, and each other.
